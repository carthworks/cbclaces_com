We are a leading Manufacturer of Jute Twine Rope, Natural Jute Rope, Braided Jute Rope, Jute Ropes Twines, Jute Yarn Twines Ropes and Twisted Burlap Jute Twine Rope from Chennai, India.

At Chennai Braiding Company, we specialize in crafting high-quality shoe laces for a wide range of footwear applications. With over 18 years of industry experience, we are proud to be one of the leading manufacturers and suppliers of durable, stylish, and customizable shoe laces in India. Founded in 2007 and based in Kanchipuram, Tamil Nadu.
Our commitment to quality and customer satisfaction has earned us a reputation as a trusted partner in the footwear industry. We cater to various segments, including sports, fashion, and industrial footwear, providing laces that meet the highest standards of durability and design.

About Us

Learn More About Us

Chennai Braiding Company is a trusted name in the shoe lace manufacturing industry, with 18 years of experience and excellence. Established in 2007, we are a proprietorship firm based in Kanchipuram, Tamil Nadu. Our company specializes in the manufacturing of all types of high-quality shoe laces, catering to a wide range of customer needs across different segments.

With a commitment to quality, durability, and innovation, we have consistently delivered products that meet international standards. Over the years, we have built a reputation for timely delivery, customized solutions, and customer-centric services.

We are proud to be recognized as a Micro Enterprise under the MSME classification for the financial year 2024–2025.

We manufacture a wide variety of shoe laces to suit every style and function:

  • Round Shoe Laces
  • Flat Shoe Laces
  • Waxed Shoe Laces
  • Fashion & Designer Laces
  • Customized Laces (as per client requirements)

Whether you are a footwear brand, wholesaler, or retailer, we provide bulk production with consistent quality and timely delivery.
